How it works

From daily log to verified record in four steps

Built around one rule: once an entry is approved, nobody — not the student, not the supervisor, not even us — can quietly change it.

Students log daily

Activities, hours, and skills recorded online from anywhere — drafts save automatically until submitted.

Supervisors review remotely

Industry supervisors approve entries or return them with comments — no paper logbook to chase or sign.

Entries lock on approval

Approved entries are sealed with a cryptographic hash. Any tampering afterwards is immediately detectable.

Reports verify with a QR

Final reports generate automatically with a QR code — universities and employers verify authenticity instantly.

Integrity by design

Paper logbooks can be rewritten. This one can't.

The Interns Ledger treats every approved entry like a sealed envelope: its contents are hashed at approval, chained into the student's record, and exposed for public verification — so a forged or edited logbook simply doesn't check out.
